## Framework / data-quality controls

1. External colleague’s 0–10 scores and +0.3/-0.3 antifragility modifiers **are not Alpha Model inputs**. Research Priority ≠ stock ranking.
2. Preserve original as-of dates, fiscal year, currency, adjusted-vs-GAAP EPS, price security (ADR vs local shares), and source version.
3. Operating Drivers → Revenue → Margin → EBIT/Net Income → EPS/FCF required before changing Our Base.
4. A positive revenue surprise is not proof of outperformance vs consensus without the pre-release forecast.
5. Analyst price target ≠ Expectation Gap. Gap compares **same-basis Our Base / Market Expectation − 1**.
6. “Antifragility” needs a **stress → mechanism → operating KPI → FCF/EPS impact** chain. No automatic modifier.
7. **SPCX identity hold:** do not treat a ticker/price as SpaceX common equity without verified instrument mapping.
8. This review is a **source-of-truth research memo**, not an immutable weekly snapshot. Do not backfill Oct 12 snapshots from a colleague’s model.
